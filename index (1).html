International Partnerships

Why Veterans India works with organisations beyond its borders, and what that collaboration is meant to achieve.

Veterans India partners with organisations abroad to widen support for veterans and their families, exchange practice on rehabilitation and resettlement, and connect Indian veterans' welfare work to a global community facing similar challenges. Every partnership is judged by the difference it makes to a veteran's daily life — not by the size of the logo on a page.

— Office of the Founder & National President
